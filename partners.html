Partners

Our partners

Our work is only possible with local schools, foundations, hospitals, universities and associations who welcome us, work alongside us and continue the care when we leave. These are the partners who stand with us.

Signed agreement

SLB Negeri 1 Denpasar

Public special-education school · Denpasar, Bali, Indonesia

SLB Negeri 1 Denpasar is a public special-education school in Bali. Since our first missions, we have provided free therapy for its students and training for its staff.

Signed agreement

Yayasan De Legong Anak Bangsa

Foundation (yayasan) · Gianyar, Bali, Indonesia

Yayasan De Legong Anak Bangsa in Gianyar, Bali, cares for children with disabilities, especially cerebral palsy. Together we provide free therapy, family support and better facilities.

Signed agreement

Yayasan Mentari Fajar

Foundation (yayasan) and inclusive school · Jimbaran, Bali, Indonesia

Yayasan Mentari Fajar runs an inclusive school and therapy programme for children with special needs in Jimbaran, Bali. Our teams provide therapy and training alongside its staff.

Visit website ↗
Signed agreement

Universitas Dhyana Pura

Private university · Badung, Bali, Indonesia

Universitas Dhyana Pura is a university in Bali. Through a signed agreement, we work together on training, student participation and building local rehabilitation capacity.

Visit website ↗
Collaboration

Center of Complex Rehabilitation “Halychyna”

State rehabilitation centre · Lviv, Ukraine

The Halychyna Center of Complex Rehabilitation in Lviv is a state rehabilitation centre for veterans and civilians. Our teams work alongside its professionals during our rehabilitation missions in Ukraine.

Visit website ↗
Collaboration

Novovolynsk Central City Hospital

Municipal public hospital · Novovolynsk, Volyn, Ukraine

Novovolynsk Central City Hospital is a municipal hospital in Volyn, Ukraine. It collaborates with Hands With Heart on rehabilitation care for people affected by disability and war.

Visit website ↗
Signed agreement

Asociación Dawe Ese Wakpa Kimoie

Indigenous civil association · Talamanca, Limón, Costa Rica

Asociación Dawe Ese Wakpa Kimoie promotes projects for Indigenous people with disabilities in Talamanca, Costa Rica. Together we bring care to families in remote Bribri communities.

Collaboration

Western University of Health Sciences

Private university · Pomona, California, United States

Western University of Health Sciences is a graduate university of health professions in California. We collaborate on osteopathic education, student participation and adaptive sport.

Visit website ↗

We list partners only where there is a signed agreement or a documented collaboration, and we say which. Logos are added as each partner gives permission.
