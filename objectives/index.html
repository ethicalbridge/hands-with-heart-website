Our objectives

Five objectives. One set of systems to make them last.

Between 2026 and 2029 our aim is to scale up free, repeated, respectful disability care, and the local capacity to sustain it, on systems strong enough to carry it.

External objectives

What we will scale up for people and communities

ESO1 · Care

Free healthcare for people with disabilities

Children and adults with disabilities in underserved settings receive the specialised care they need, free of charge, and receive it more than once.

ESO2 · Knowledge

Training and mentorship for local healthcare professionals

Our goal is not only to provide care, but to leave knowledge behind.

ESO3 · Permanence

Sustainable community-based healthcare solutions

Our goal is to solve the structural weakness of mission-based care: continuity.

ESO4 · Advocacy

Change social perceptions and barriers towards people with disabilities

Care alone cannot reach a child who is kept at home. The barriers people with disabilities face are often social before they are clinical.

ESO5 · Sustainability

Diversify sources of funds and build strong partnerships for sustainability

Our goal is to fund the mission for the long term, while keeping care free for the people we serve.

Internal objectives

Systems for scale

Underneath all five sits the organisational capability that makes growth safe, fundable and sustainable: governance, safeguarding, evidence, people and communication. Every step up in scale must be matched by a step up in systems.

Read about our systems

Our scaling principle

We grow only as fast as our governance, safeguarding and evidence allow. We move to the next stage only when the systems for it are in place.

Our theory of change

Our aim, our condition, our first test

Our aim

Scale up free, repeated, respectful disability care, and the local capacity to sustain it.

Our condition

Systems before scale. We grow only as fast as our governance, safeguarding and evidence allow.

Our first test

Can we launch Bahía de Niños on a legal, locally led and funded footing while missions continue?
